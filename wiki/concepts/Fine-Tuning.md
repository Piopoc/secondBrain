# Fine-Tuning

The second phase of training for large models, occurring after [[Pre-Training]].

## Process
The pre-trained model is further trained on a smaller, labeled dataset for a specific task (e.g., sentiment analysis or translation). This leverages the general knowledge acquired during pre-training to achieve high performance on the target task.
