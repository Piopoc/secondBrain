# Information Retrieval (IR)

**Information Retrieval** is the science of searching for information in a collection of unstructured data (usually text). Unlike database queries, which seek an exact match, IR seeks the **most relevant** items to satisfy a user's information need.

### Core Characteristics
- **Unstructured Data**: Operates on free text, images, or audio.
- **Best Match**: Results are ranked by relevance rather than being a binary "found/not found".
- **Iterative Process**: Users often refine their queries based on the results they receive.
