# Distributional Hypothesis

The **Distributional Hypothesis** is the theoretical foundation of modern word embeddings. It posits that **words that occur in the same contexts tend to have similar meanings**.

## Implementation in IR
In practical terms, this means that if two words are frequently surrounded by the same set of other words, they can be represented by similar vectors in a latent space.

## Relation to Embeddings
This hypothesis allows the transition from counting co-occurrences (sparse) to learning latent representations (dense) by training models to predict a word given its context (or vice versa).
