# Ontology

An **Ontology** is a formal way of representing a domain of knowledge. In the context of the Semantic Web, it defines the abstract categories (concepts) and the relationships that exist between them.

## Role in the Semantic Web
Ontologies sit at the highest layer of semantic representation. They provide the "schema" or conceptual framework that governs how data is structured. For example, an ontology for biology would define the concept of "Species" and the relationship "is a subspecies of".

## Web Ontology Language (OWL)
**OWL (Web Ontology Language)** is the standard language used to express ontologies on the web. It allows for:
- **Precise Semantics:** Defining properties and constraints.
- **Reasoning:** Enabling systems to perform classification, consistency checking, and logical inference.
