# NER (Named Entity Recognition)

**NER** is a subtask of Information Extraction that seeks to locate and classify named entities mentioned in unstructured text into predefined categories.

## Common Categories
- **Person**: e.g., "Albert Einstein".
- **Location**: e.g., "Rome".
- **Organization**: e.g., "Google".

## Relation to IR
NER allows a system to move from simple keyword matching to **entity-based retrieval**, where the system understands that "Apple" in a specific context refers to the company and not the fruit.
