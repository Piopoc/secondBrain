# Jaccard Similarity

A statistic used for measuring the similarity and diversity of sample sets.

## Definition
The size of the intersection divided by the size of the union of the sample sets.
$$J(A, B) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|}$$

## Usage in IR
Used to compare the overlap between the set of tokens in an original query and an offuscated query.
