# BM25 (Best Match 25)

**BM25** is a state-of-the-art ranking function used in many modern search engines. It is an evolution of the [[Binary-Independence-Model]].

## Key Improvements over TF-IDF
1. **TF Saturation**: Unlike linear TF, BM25 recognizes that the benefit of seeing a term multiple times diminishes. After a few occurrences, the score plateaus.
2. **Document Length Normalization**: It penalizes very long documents that might contain a term many times simply because they are long, rather than because they are highly relevant.

## Parameters
- $k_1$: Controls the saturation of the term frequency.
- $b$: Controls the degree of length normalization.
