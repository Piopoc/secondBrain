# Tau di Kendall

A non-parametric measure of the correspondence between two rankings.

## Usage in IR
Used to test the **stability of the pool**. If removing one system's contribution to the pool doesn't significantly change the ranking of other systems (measured by $\tau$), the pool is considered stable.
