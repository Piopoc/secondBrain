# POS Tagging

**POS (Part-of-Speech) Tagging** is the process of marking up a word in a text as corresponding to a particular part of speech, based on both its definition and its context.

## Implementation
It typically uses statistical models (like Hidden Markov Models or Neural Networks) to predict tags such as:
- `NN`: Noun
- `VB`: Verb
- `JJ`: Adjective

## Use in IR
POS tagging can be used to filter queries (e.g., ignoring adjectives to focus on nouns) or to improve the precision of NER.
