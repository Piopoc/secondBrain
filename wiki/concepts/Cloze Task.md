# Cloze Task

A test of vocabulary and comprehension where a word or phrase is removed from a passage, and the reader is asked to fill in the blank.

## In Machine Learning
The Cloze task is the fundamental objective for [[Bidirectional Encoder]]s. Instead of predicting the next word, the model predicts a missing word based on the context provided by both the left and right sides of the gap.
