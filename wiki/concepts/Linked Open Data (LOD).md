# Linked Open Data (LOD)

**Linked Open Data (LOD)** is Linked Data that is published under an open license, ensuring that the data can be freely reused, redistributed, and integrated by anyone.

## The LOD Cloud
The **Linked Open Data Cloud** is a visual map of the interconnected datasets available on the web. It is organized by domain (e.g., Geography, Life Sciences, Government) and shows how datasets like **DBpedia** and **Wikidata** serve as central hubs connecting diverse information sources.
