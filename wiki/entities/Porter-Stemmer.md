# Porter-Stemmer

The **Porter Stemmer** is one of the most widely used stemming algorithms in IR.

## Mechanism
It is a **rule-based** algorithm that applies a series of cascading rules to remove common English suffixes. It operates in several phases, always attempting to remove the longest matching suffix first.

## Characteristics
- Simple and fast.
- Does not use a dictionary.
- Prone to over-stemming in some cases.
