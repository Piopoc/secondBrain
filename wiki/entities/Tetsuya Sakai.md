# Tetsuya Sakai

**Tetsuya Sakai** is an IR researcher who has contributed to the debate on evaluation guidelines.

- **Key Position (2020):** He responded to Norbert Fuhr's guidelines, arguing that the community should not be overly prescriptive.
- **Arguments:** He pointed out that other scientific communities average ordinal measures and that measures like Reciprocal Rank (RR) align well with user behavior and intuition.
