# TIPSTER

**TIPSTER** is a classic dataset used in the early days of Information Retrieval research, particularly for the TREC conferences.

## Characteristics
- **Nature**: Composed of long-form documents, primarily news articles.
- **Focus**: Evaluating traditional lexical retrieval and the impact of stemming and stopword removal.
- **Impact**: Served as the primary benchmark for testing the effectiveness of different stemmers (Porter, Lovins, etc.) and the BM25 model.
