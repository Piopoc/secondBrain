# Norbert Fuhr

**Norbert Fuhr** is an IR researcher known for his critical perspective on evaluation practices.

- **Key Position (2017):** In his SIGIR Forum paper, he argued against the common practice of averaging measures that are not on an interval scale (such as RR or AP).
- **Theoretical Basis:** He maintains that if a measure is only ordinal, the arithmetic mean is not invariant under permissible transformations and is therefore meaningless.
