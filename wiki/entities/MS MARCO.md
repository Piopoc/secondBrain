# MS MARCO

**MS MARCO (Microsoft Machine Reading Comprehension)** is a large-scale dataset designed for training and evaluating neural information retrieval systems.

## Characteristics
- **Nature**: Consists of real-world queries and passages from the web.
- **Focus**: Specifically designed for passage retrieval and question answering.
- **Impact**: It is the benchmark dataset for modern Neural Retrievers (e.g., BERT-based models), where dense embeddings significantly outperform traditional sparse models like BM25.
