# C. J. van Rijsbergen

**C. J. "Keith" van Rijsbergen** is a prominent figure in the field of Information Retrieval.

- **Key Observation (1981):** He noted that unlike physical sciences, IR lacks a natural empirical ordering for retrieval effectiveness.
- **Impact:** This observation highlights that IR measures are artificial constructions, making the determination of their scale properties a complex theoretical challenge.
