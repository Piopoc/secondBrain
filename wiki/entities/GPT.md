# GPT

**GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer)** is a family of decoder-only Transformer models designed for autoregressive text generation.

## Characteristics
- **Causal Architecture**: Uses masked self-attention to ensure that the prediction for a token depends only on previous tokens.
- **Pre-training**: Trained on massive datasets to predict the next token.
- **Evolution**: From GPT-1 to GPT-4, the models have scaled significantly in terms of parameters and training data, leading to emergent capabilities in few-shot and zero-shot learning.
