# S. S. Stevens

**S. S. Stevens** was a psychologist who fundamentally influenced the field of measurement.

- **Key Contribution:** In his 1946 paper *"On the Theory of Scales of Measurement"*, he proposed the classification of measurement scales into four types: **Nominal, Ordinal, Interval, and Ratio**.
- **Impact:** This classification provides the basis for determining which statistical operations are permissible for a given type of data.
